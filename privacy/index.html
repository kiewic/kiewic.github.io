Waffle Chart Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2, 2016

The Power BI Waffle Chart visualization does not collect, store, or share personal information.

The visual does not send your data to external services and does not use cookies, analytics, or tracking technologies.

Data displayed by the visual is processed only within the Power BI environment in which the visual is used.

If this policy changes, this page will be updated.
